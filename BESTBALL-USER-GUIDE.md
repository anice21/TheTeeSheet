# Best Ball Format - User Guide

## What is Best Ball?

**Best Ball** (also called "Better Ball") is a team golf format where 2 or more players form a team. On each hole, the team's score is the **lowest score** among all team members.

### Example:
- Player A scores **4** on Hole 1  
- Player B scores **5** on Hole 1  
- **Team Score = 4** (the best score)

---

## Starting a Best Ball Round

### Step 1: Select Trip and Course
1. Navigate to the "Current Round" view
2. Select your trip
3. Select the course you'll be playing

### Step 2: Choose Format
1. Under "Round Type", select Full/Front 9/Back 9
2. Under **"Format"**, select **"Best Ball"**

### Step 3: Select Players
1. Choose the players who will be participating
2. You can select 1-4 players total

### Step 4: Create Teams

**Option A: Single Team** (all players on one team)
1. Click **"+ Add Team"**
2. Use the dropdown to add Player 1 to the team
3. Use the dropdown to add Player 2 to the team
4. Continue for all players
5. Click **"Start Round"**

**Option B: Multiple Teams** (e.g., 2 teams of 2)
1. Click **"+ Add Team"** → Team 1 created
2. Add Player A to Team 1
3. Add Player B to Team 1
4. Click **"+ Add Team"** again → Team 2 created
5. Add Player C to Team 2
6. Add Player D to Team 2
7. Click **"Start Round"**

**Option C: Solo Teams** (1 player per team for Nassau)
1. Click **"+ Add Team"** → Team 1 created
2. Add Player A to Team 1
3. Click **"+ Add Team"** → Team 2 created
4. Add Player B to Team 2
5. Click **"Start Round"**

### Important Notes:
- All selected players must be assigned to a team before starting
- Each team must have at least 1 player
- You can remove players from teams using the "Remove" button
- You can delete entire teams using the "Remove Team" button

## During the Round

### Entering Scores
1. Enter scores normally for each player
2. The system automatically calculates the team score
3. **Visual indicators show which score counts:**
   - ⭐ **Star icon** appears next to the player(s) with the best score
   - Best score players are shown in **bold green text**

### Example Display:
```
Hole 5 - Par 4

⭐ Player A: 4  (best score - bold green)
  Player B: 5
⭐ Player C: 4  (tied for best - bold green)
  Player D: 6

┌─────────────────────┐
│ Team Score: 4       │
│ To Par: E           │
└─────────────────────┘
```

### Tied Scores:
- If multiple players tie for the best score, all receive the star (⭐)
- The tied score counts once for the team

## Viewing Scorecards

### How to View:
1. Go to the **"Scorecard"** view
2. Select the course
3. Select the group

### What You'll See:
- Individual scores for each player
- **Team row** at the bottom showing best scores
- ⭐ Stars next to scores that count for the team
- Best scores highlighted in **bold green**

### Scorecard Example:
```
| Hole  | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | Out | Total |
|-------|---|---|---|---|-----|-------|
| Par   | 4 | 3 | 5 | 4 | 36  | 72    |
| Alice |⭐4| 4 | 6 | 5 | 38  | 78    |
| Bob   | 5 |⭐3|⭐5|⭐4| 36  | 74    |
| Team  | 4 | 3 | 5 | 4 | 36  | 74    |
```

**Reading the Scorecard:**
- Alice's score of **4** on Hole 1 is the team's best score (⭐)
- Bob's score of **3** on Hole 2 is the team's best score (⭐)
- Bob's scores on Holes 3 and 4 are also best (⭐)
- Team total is **74** (best score from each hole)

## Viewing the Leaderboard

### Best Ball Leaderboard:
1. Go to **"Leaderboard"** view
2. Select the course
3. For best ball rounds, you'll automatically see team standings

### Team Leaderboard Shows:
- **Rank** - Team's position
- **Team** - Team name (or Team 1, Team 2, etc.)
- **Players** - Names of all team members
- **Score** - Team's total score (sum of best scores per hole)
- **To Par** - How many strokes above/below par

### Individual Rounds:
- For individual format rounds, the leaderboard shows player standings as usual

## Round Summary

After finishing a round, you'll see:

### Team Summary Panel:
```
┌─────────────────────────────────────┐
│ Team Score: 74                      │
│ To Par: +2                          │
│                                     │
│ Individual Contributions            │
│                                     │
│ Alice                               │
│ Holes Won: 8 | Individual: 78      │
│                                     │
│ Bob                                 │
│ Holes Won: 10 | Individual: 74     │
└─────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### Understanding Contributions:
- **Holes Won** = Number of holes where that player had the best score
- **Individual Total** = Player's own total score (not team score)
- Shows which player contributed more to the team's success

## Common Scenarios

### Scenario 1: Classic Best Ball (4 Players, 1 Team)
**Use Case:** Four friends playing together as one team  
**Setup:** Add all 4 players to Team 1  
**Result:** One team score calculated from the best of 4 scores per hole

### Scenario 2: Competitive Match (4 Players, 2 Teams)
**Use Case:** Two vs Two competition  
**Setup:**  
- Team 1: Player A + Player B
- Team 2: Player C + Player D

**Result:**  
- Each team has a score calculated from their 2 players
- Leaderboard shows which team is winning
- All 4 players still play together in the same group

### Scenario 3: Nassau Match (2 Players, 2 Solo Teams)
**Use Case:** Head-to-head match play with best ball scoring  
**Setup:**  
- Team 1: Player A only
- Team 2: Player B only

**Result:**  
- Each player's score IS their team score
- Useful for tracking Nassau bets (front 9, back 9, overall)

### Scenario 4: Mixed Format Practice Round
**Use Case:** One player practicing alone as a "team"  
**Setup:** Team 1 with just 1 player  
**Result:** Solo practice round tracked as best ball format

## Tips & Best Practices

### Team Creation:
- ✅ Create teams before starting the round
- ✅ Ensure all players are assigned to a team
- ✅ Team names default to "Team 1", "Team 2", etc. (no need to enter custom names)

- ❌ Don't leave players unassigned

- ✅ Enter scores normally - system calculates team score automatically

### Viewing Results:
- ✅ Leaderboard automatically shows team standings for best ball rounds
- ✅ Check Round Summary for detailed contribution stats
- ✅ Scorecard shows exactly which scores counted for team

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: Can I have more than 2 teams?
**A:** Yes! You can have up to 4 teams (limited by 4 players maximum per group for pace of play).

### Q: Can teams have different numbers of players?
**A:** Yes! You could have a team of 1 player competing against a team of 3 players. However, the team with more players may have an advantage.

### Q: What happens if players tie for best score?
**A:** Both players receive a ⭐ indicator, but the score only counts once for the team.

### Q: Can I switch players between teams after starting?
**A:** No, team assignments are locked once the round starts. If you need to change teams, you'll need to discard the round and start over.

### Q: Can I view old best ball rounds?
**A:** Yes! Go to Scorecard view, select the course and group. Team scores are permanently saved.

### Q: Do handicaps apply to best ball?
**A:** In the current version, handicaps are tracked but not applied to team scores. This is a potential future enhancement.

### Q: Can I mix individual and best ball rounds in a tournament?
**A:** Yes! You can have some rounds in individual format and others in best ball format within the same trip/tournament.

## Troubleshooting

### Problem: "Please add at least one team" error
**Solution:** Click the "+ Add Team" button to create a team before starting.

### Problem: "All teams must have at least one player" error
**Solution:** Make sure every team you created has at least one player assigned. Remove empty teams or add players to them.

### Problem: "X player(s) not assigned to any team" error
**Solution:** Use the dropdowns to add all selected players to teams. Every player must be on a team.

### Problem: I don't see team scores on the leaderboard
**Solution:** Team scores only appear for rounds played in Best Ball format. Individual rounds show player standings.

### Problem: Team score doesn't match what I calculated
**Solution:**  
- Check that you're looking at the correct holes (front 9, back 9, or full)
- Verify you're using the BEST (lowest) score per hole
- Remember: 0 scores don't count (hole not yet played)

### Problem: Star (⭐) not showing
**Solution:**  
- Stars only appear when a score is entered AND is the best score
- If all players have the same score, all get stars
- Stars don't show for holes not yet played (score = 0)
